# Research and positioning

Checked **3 October 2026, America/Phoenix**. Browser timestamps may show 4 October UTC. Product facts are drawn from primary sources. Design judgments below are creative recommendations, not measured outcomes.

## Recommendation

Keep **BetterTile**. Lead with a visible shared boundary, then adaptive Bento and Tabbed. Free ownership is reassurance, not a unique category claim. Rectangle, Loop, AeroSpace, and Amethyst also offer free open-source software. Rectangle Pro documents adjacent resizing on release and saved-layout automation. BetterTile can confidently demonstrate its direct seam, live/ghost choice, and adaptive layout behavior without saying it is first or exclusive.

**Positioning:** For Mac users working across several windows, BetterTile keeps the workspace aligned as work changes. Direct linked resizing, adaptive Bento, and familiar placement controls reduce repeated arranging. The experience should feel precise, calm, capable, and approachable.

## Identity implications from actual icons

Category visuals are documented in the [category report](category.md). Magnet already uses a white three-pane shape on blue; BetterSnapTool uses four silver panes; Rectangle uses a window silhouette; Loop uses a ring. Avoiding those broad constructions matters more than choosing an unusual gradient.

The proposed **Shared Edge** mark uses two open brackets and a taller central rail. Its negative space is visible at small sizes. The blue/emerald materials connect to the existing BetterTile icon; the core one-color drawing remains independent of those materials. This is an original design proposal, not an exclusive-rights claim.

## Three directions and selection

1. **Shared Edge — selected:** pitch black, cobalt gradients, teal; Inter display and system-style product windows. One shared rail ties together logo, active divider and paired page compositions. Best alignment with linked resizing and the requested dark default.
2. **Open Space:** cool white, ink and ultramarine; editorial Instrument Serif; offset adaptable frame. Calmer editorial alternative, less connected to the requested dark identity.
3. **Quiet Instrument:** black, silver and slate; compact system sans with mono labels; joined initials. Precise but more technical and less welcoming.

The [brand preview](../brand.html) contains style tiles with type, palette, logo sketches and hero compositions. [Brand guide](../BRAND-GUIDE.md) develops the selected system.

## Final media and atmosphere direction

The working website combines the user's final preferences: Sigma's black-to-blue atmosphere, Atlas's large integrated media presentation, and Shotbase's attention to card materials and clear sections. It retains only the opening linked-resize interaction. The remaining features use real captures or clearly marked review slots. No pricing plans, testimonials, customer counts or unsupported features were added.

[Shotbase](https://shotbase.com/) was inspected at 1440×1000 and 375×812. Observed: compact dark navigation; white abstract symbol; TWK Everett Medium measured in the DOM; atmospheric hero with a playing background video; large rounded recording frame; alternating feature copy and gradient-backed media cards; cropped UI captures; broad editor comparison surface; stacked mobile cards. Its screenshot card showed a tap-to-capture prompt on mobile. A full motion audit was not performed. BetterTile borrows the care in framing and segmentation, not its colors, artwork, paid plans or distinctive composition.

Atlas was revisited at the supplied landingfolio URL. The large product demonstration has a colored rounded outer frame, a scenario strip, and a prominent interface surface. This supports larger media areas with modest framing; it does not require recreating Atlas's simulated app or character.
